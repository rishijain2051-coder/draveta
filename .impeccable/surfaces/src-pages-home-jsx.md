## Direction contract

THESIS: Everything is built in front of you from the first line. Construction geometry (datum lines, compass arcs, 45° diagonals, dimension ticks) draws the Draveta knot, then keeps drafting and renders real working product UI. Refuses the category default: stock-photo hero, stat row, same-size feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drafting film (cool, faintly blue-grey white) with a fine grid; construction lines in non-photo blue, finished work in ink black; ultramarine as the committed "built" colour that owns whole fields (CTA, closing band, active states). Mona Sans, wide and black for display; weight rises from hairline to black as text "inks". Martian Mono only for measurements, codes and data. Components are drafted: hairline frames with dimension ticks, a drafting title block for credentials, no rounded soft cards, no icon tiles, no emoji.

STORY: A visitor sees a company literally construct its own mark and its own software in seconds, understands Draveta builds anything from scratch, sees six products already running a real industry, and calls or WhatsApps.
